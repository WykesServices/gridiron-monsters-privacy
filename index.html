What we collect

Nothing. Gridiron Monsters does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device ID, or any other personal information.

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party trackers.
  • We never sell or share data, because we have none.
  • The app works offline and sends no data to us.

Your save

Your game progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your save. A save code contains only your game progress, so share it only with people you trust with your save.

Purchases

Optional Trophy Packs are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles every payment under their own privacy policies. We never see your name, email, or payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase went through. Refund requests go to Apple or Google.

Children

The game is meant for players 13 and older. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission to make purchases.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including Texas and California, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold and to have it deleted. We hold none. To erase your save, use Restart game in Settings or delete the app.

Fictional players

Every player, name, and stat in Gridiron Monsters is fictional. The game is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or licensed by the NFL, the NFLPA, any team, or any player.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
